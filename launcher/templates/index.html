The Bibliographic Way

          
Workflow: PDF Literature ➔ PyPottery Lens ➔ Digital Vessel Cards

        

      

      
    


    

      

        

          Bibliographic Workflow
          
PDF ➔ PyPottery Lens ➔ Vessel Cards

          

            Transform legacy archaeological publications, excavation monographs, and typological PDF catalogs into structured digital vessel cards ready for research, cataloging, and analysis.
          

        


        
        

          
          

            

              
📄

              

            

            

              

                Step 01
                Source Literature
              

              
PDF Document Ingestion

              

                Start with any archaeological publication in PDF format — excavation reports, monographs, journal articles, or scanned plate catalogs. Multi-page documents are indexed and segmented into individual page figures for analysis.
              

              

                PDF / Scanned Monographs
                Plate Indexing
                Publication Metadata
              

            

          


          
          

            

              
🔍

              

            

            

              

                Step 02
                PyPottery Lens
              

              
PyPottery Lens Processing

              

                PyPottery Lens analyzes the PDF pages using computer vision AI models. It automatically detects and isolates individual vessel profile drawings, section cuts, rim orientations, scale bars (cm/m), figure captions, and artifact labels.
              

              

                AI Profile Extraction
                Automatic Scale Bar Detection
                Section & Rim Alignment
              

            

          


          
          

            

              
🏺

            

            

              

                Step 03
                Digital Output
              

              
Digital Vessel Cards Generation

              

                Every extracted ceramic drawing is transformed into a standardized, structured "Digital Vessel Card". Each card bundles the isolated profile image, scale measurements, catalog references, and archaeological context — ready to launch into vector inking, profiling, or database export.
              

              

                Standardized Vessel Cards
                Calibrated Measurements
                Ready for Suite Analysis
